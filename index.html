Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding flatmates across Kolkata.

Is FlatMate Kolkata completely free to use?
Yes! Browsing, filtering profiles, and posting your flatmate requirements are 100% free with zero brokerage.
How do you verify users on the platform?
We verify users through corporate email addresses, LinkedIn profiles, and identity authentication to ensure a secure community.
Can I search specifically in IT hubs like Salt Lake Sector V or New Town?
Absolutely. We have specialized filters covering major Kolkata residential & IT belts including New Town Action Areas I-III, Salt Lake Sector I-V, Rajarhat, and South Kolkata hubs like Gariahat and Tollygunge.
How do I contact potential flatmates?
Clicking "Contact" immediately routes you to direct WhatsApp communication with complete flatmate preferences attached for quick coordination.
